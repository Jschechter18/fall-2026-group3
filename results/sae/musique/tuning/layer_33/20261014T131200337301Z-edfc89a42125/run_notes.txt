- trial 7: has the most sparcity

- key configs:
    - sparcity_coefficient = 5
- selected because:
    - pretty good or at least reasonable reconstruction (relative to top_k = 64)
    - still has good sparsity
    - has a very high fraction of dictionary usage - indicating that most features are being seen by the model at some point

- limitations
    - there are about 70% dead features. this means that it learns only on about 30% of the features.
    - this could be a severe limitation in the causality pipeline, as some features likely are just not learned

- potential experiments to improve:
    - we like that this is both sparse and high dictionary usage, but we could work on reconstruction accuracy
    - freeze the encoder since we like the sparcity and dictionary usage, but fine tune the decoder/dictionary to improve its ability to reconstruction
    - benefits of this experiment:
        - it could improve the distortion between the ablated groups and the non-intervened groups, allowing for better controls in the causality pipeline
    - potential issues with this experiment:
        - it could result in damaging our interventions
